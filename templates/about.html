Our Story

Addo Mart is a retail business committed to providing quality products at affordable prices. From everyday groceries and beverages to household essentials, we stock a wide range of items to meet the needs of our customers.

Founded by Addo Richmond, our mission is simple: make shopping easy, reliable, and enjoyable for every customer.

Our Mission

To deliver quality products with excellent customer service, ensuring that every visit to Addo Mart is a pleasant one.

Our Values

  • Quality: We carefully select the products we sell.
  • Integrity: We are honest and transparent in all we do.
  • Customer First: Our customers are at the heart of our business.
  • Community: We love supporting the community we serve.
